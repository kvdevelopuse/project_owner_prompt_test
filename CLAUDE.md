# Project rules

- The scope is exactly `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md`. Do not add features beyond it (no edit/delete, reports, export, login or fees tracking unless the owner asks).
- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step, no backend.
- All data is stored in the browser's localStorage.
- The app must open by double-clicking `index.html` (works from `file://`).
- No new dependencies without asking the owner.
- Ask the owner before any product or design decision that the requirements do not settle.
